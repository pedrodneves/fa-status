# FA Status Portal

A public page where Featured App applicants check the status of their own FA applications and DVP requests, without seeing anyone else's. Applicants enter the email address they applied with and receive a private link to their applications. The page also lists, for everyone, the apps that hold Featured App status and those whose status was revoked, with their Party IDs (also as JSON and CSV).

The data comes from the Featured App Submissions and Asset Moving Status Requests boards on Monday, read-only.

- `docs/`: the page (GitHub Pages)
- `apps-script/`: the Google Apps Script backend
